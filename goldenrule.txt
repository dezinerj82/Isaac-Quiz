ACTIVE QUIZ DESIGN
Responsive, white-background, pastel rainbow Quiz time branding, rounded friendly fonts, subject/volume menu, timer, progress, question counter, Exit and Next controls. Question x out of x appears above the question card on the left; timer button widened. Next is disabled until answered and only advances. Exit returns to subject selection. A typed answer, where present in other future quizzes, submits with Enter; a selected answer submits on tap; sorting submits when all steps selected; matching submits when all selections complete. Korean and Science Exam Revision use NO TYPING; former typed questions were converted to selectable multiple-choice blanks. Sorting supports tap and drag/drop. Picture questions use visible illustrations or symbols.

BOOKMARKS AND REVIEW
Bookmark checkbox appears beside question count. Bookmark list button appears when bookmarks exist. Clicking a bookmarked question navigates to it. List closes after 3 seconds or outside click, but inside-list clicks MUST NOT trigger premature closing (exclude #bookmarkList from outside-pointer handler). Answered questions can be reviewed without altering their saved results. Resume returns to the first unanswered/current continuation position. Do not regress this navigation fix.

TIMER AND RESULTS

On finish, show the score image above Quiz Complete: >=90% images/Isaac high score.png; >=70% images/Isaac middle score.png; >=50% images/Isaac low score.png; <50% images/Isaac fail.png. Keep Try Again, Back Home and Download Score. Score export includes subject, volume, score, date and incorrect question numbers/text, not personal data uploads.
